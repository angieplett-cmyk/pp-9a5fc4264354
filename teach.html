🛡️ R · Responsible

Responsable

👀 Looks like

Materials out and ready

Doing my job in the pod

Cleaning up my space

👂 Sounds like

"I've got the supplies."

"Let's finish on time."

"Yo traigo los materiales."

💗 E · Empathy

Empatía

👀 Looks like

Helping a pod mate who is stuck

Kind face, eyes on the speaker

Everyone gets a turn

👂 Sounds like

"Are you okay?"

"Want me to help?"

"¿Estás bien? ¿Te ayudo?"

🧘 M · Mindfulness

Atención plena

👀 Looks like

Calm body, on task

Taking a breath before I talk

Thinking deeply about the question

👂 Sounds like

Quiet voices

"Let me think…"

"Déjame pensar…"

⭐ I · Integrity

Integridad

👀 Looks like

Doing the right thing when no one is watching

Honest work, my own ideas

👂 Sounds like

"That was my mistake. I'll fix it."

"Let's be fair."

"Fue mi error. Lo arreglo."
